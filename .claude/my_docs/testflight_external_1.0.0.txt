Beta App Description (до 4000 знаков):

Line Duty is a one-finger arcade about dispatching traffic. Units of four colours roll in from the top; draw a route with your finger and lead each one into the gate of its colour. The route erases behind the unit, a red ring warns when two get too close, and a collision or a wrong gate ends the run. One free continue per run. Difficulty ramps up with every delivery.

What to test in this build:
• Drawing routes: the line should follow your finger without lag; leading it into a gate of the same colour docks the route and you can lift your finger.
• Five visual themes (Metro, City, Aquarium, Anthill, Garden) — switch them from the strip in the main menu; everything should recolour instantly, including the pause screen and settings.
• Bonuses: hexagons appear on the field about every 20 seconds. Drive a unit through one — Freeze, Shield, Points ×2 or Autopilot. Check the indicators under the score and the badge.
• "How to play" tutorial on first launch and again from Settings.
• Sound, vibration and language (English / Русский) in Settings; the best score and statistics should survive restarting the app.

No account or sign-in is needed. The game works fully offline and contains no ads or purchases.

Please report anything that feels off: routes that don't dock, collisions that look unfair, units stuck at the edges, wrong colours after switching a theme, text that overflows, crashes.
